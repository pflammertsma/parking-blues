1. What the Service is

Parking Blues suggests places where on-street parking is likely to be legal in Zurich, based on public open data and on the rules for blue and white zones. The City of Zurich does not publish live occupancy for on-street spaces, so the Service cannot know whether a space is free. Time limits, fees and the position of spaces are estimates and may be outdated, incomplete or wrong.
